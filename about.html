Our mission

One platform. Infinite possibilities.

Founded in 2019, Jupiter began as a small team of engineers and designers frustrated by fragmented workflows. Today we power operations for over 10,000 companies across 120 countries.

We believe software should disappear into the work — fast, reliable, and quietly powerful.

  • Customer-first — every roadmap decision starts with user feedback.
  • Remote-native — a distributed team across 14 time zones.
  • Carbon neutral — our infrastructure runs on 100% renewable energy.
