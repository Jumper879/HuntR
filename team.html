OUR ORIGIN STORY

Why We Built HuntR

In 2023, our founder Babatunde spent two exhausting months searching for an apartment in Lagos. Like millions of Nigerians, he paid over ₦35,000 in non-refundable "mobilization and inspection fees" to agents who showed him phantom apartments, dilapidated homes, or properties that had already been rented weeks prior.

When he finally found a home, the middleman agent slapped a 10% agency fee and another 10% legal fee on top — demanding ₦2.8 Million simply for forwarding a landlord’s phone number. That same week in Abuja, Amina was dealing with a sub-agent syndicate that vanished with a prospective tenant's caution deposit.

We asked ourselves a simple question: Why should ordinary Nigerians pay 20% in arbitrary middleman cuts when technology can connect tenants directly with verified homeowners? HuntR was born to replace greedy cartels with everyday community scouts ("Hunters") who get paid directly for documenting vacant homes.
